This site stores nothing about you in your browser: no cookies, no local storage, no identifiers. Its only in-browser measurement relies on the consent exemption from the Spanish Data Protection Agency. That is why you will not see a cookie banner. This page explains what the site does, what measurement it uses and why the law does not require me to ask for your permission.

## Data controller

The processing described here is run by Juan José López Jiménez, who you can write to at [hello@lojopez.dev](mailto:hello@lojopez.dev). Full identification details are in the [legal notice](/en/legal-notice).

## What is stored in your browser

Nothing about you. The site writes no cookies, does not use `localStorage` or `sessionStorage`, and loads no third-party scripts that do.

Cloudflare, the company that serves the site, may ask your browser to remember a technical instruction for a few days, to report network errors to it. According to Cloudflare, those reports contain no personal data.

The language is not stored: the address of the page decides it, `/es` for Spanish and `/en` for English. If you arrive at the root of the site, I redirect you to the content available in Spanish or English. I do not store that choice.

## Why there is no cookie banner

Article 22.2 of Spanish [Law 34/2002 of 11 July (LSSI-CE)](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2002-13758), which transposes [Directive 2002/58/EC](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX%3A32002L0058) as amended by [Directive 2009/136/EC](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX%3A32009L0136), requires your consent before storing information on your device or accessing what is already there, unless it is strictly necessary for a service you asked for.

This site stores no information about you on your device. For measurement, the only thing that accesses it is the Web Analytics script, which reads technical data about the visit, such as page load times. The [Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD)](https://www.aepd.es/guias/guia-cookies-analiticas-externas.pdf) considers this measurement strictly necessary, and so not subject to consent, if it only produces anonymous statistics about the site itself, is carried out solely on behalf of its publisher, and the data is not matched with other processing, passed to third parties or reused. I use Web Analytics only for that. Cloudflare's network error instruction is technical: it only serves to detect failures in delivering the page.

That is why a cookie banner would have nothing to ask you. Asking for permission for something that does not need it would only interrupt you and teach you to click "accept" without reading.

## Measurement

The site uses two tools from Cloudflare, the company that serves it.

**Domain Analytics.** It runs on Cloudflare's servers, not in your browser. It gives traffic figures, such as requests, page views, visitors by country and errors. It runs nothing on your device.

**Web Analytics.** It measures how the site is used and how it performs, such as page views and load times, with a small script that downloads with the page. According to Cloudflare, it uses no cookies or local storage and does not identify individuals. The data it handles is described in the [privacy policy](/en/privacy).

## How to limit the measurement

If you prefer that your visit does not count in Web Analytics, you can block its script with an extension such as [uBlock Origin](https://github.com/gorhill/uBlock) or a browser that does it by default. Domain Analytics counts requests on Cloudflare's servers, so you cannot avoid it from your browser. The site works the same: nothing it offers depends on these tools.

If I ever add a feature that stores or reads data on your device and is not strictly necessary for you to use the site, I will add a cookie banner that asks for your consent before it stores or reads anything, and I will update this page.
